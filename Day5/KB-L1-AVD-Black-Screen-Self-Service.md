# My Work Desktop Shows a Black Screen After Login

## What is happening?

After signing in to your work desktop, you see a black screen instead of your normal desktop. The screen may stay black, or it may fix itself after about 30 seconds. This is a known issue and it is being worked on. **Your files, emails, and data are completely safe — nothing has been lost.**

---

## What you can try yourself

**Step 1 — Wait 30 seconds.**  
Sometimes the desktop takes a little longer to load. Give it 30 seconds before doing anything else.  
✅ If your desktop appears — you're good to go. No further action needed.

**Step 2 — Close and reopen the app.**  
If the black screen is still showing after 30 seconds, close the app you use to open your work desktop completely. Wait 10 seconds, then open it again and sign in as normal.  
✅ If your desktop appears this time — you're good to go.

**Step 3 — Try again once more.**  
If you still see a black screen, fully sign out — do not just close the window. Click your name or the Start button and choose **Sign out**. Wait 1 minute, then sign back in one more time.  
✅ If your desktop appears — you're good to go.

**Step 4 — If none of the above worked, contact the IT Help Desk.** See the section below.

---

## Your data is safe

You do not need to worry about any files or work you had open. Your documents and emails are saved safely on our systems and are not affected by this issue. When your desktop is working again, everything will be exactly as you left it.

---

## Contact the IT Help Desk if...

- The black screen is still showing after completing all three steps above
- The black screen keeps coming back every time you sign in
- Other people in your team are reporting the same problem at the same time

**Call or submit a request and mention the following:**

- Your full name and contact number
- The time the black screen first appeared (e.g. "9:05am")
- Whether the screen recovered on its own or stayed black
- How many times you have tried signing back in
- Whether anyone else on your team is affected
- The message (if any) shown on screen before it went black
