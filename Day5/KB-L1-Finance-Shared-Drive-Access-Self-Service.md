# I Cannot Open the Finance Team Folder

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Version | v 1.0 |
| Date | 07/08/2026 |
| Status | Draft |

## What this means

If your Finance team folder is missing or will not open, this usually means it did not load properly when you signed in. This can often be fixed quickly.

Your data is safe. Your files are still stored securely, and this issue does not delete or change your documents.

## Steps you can do yourself

1. Wait 60 seconds after sign-in.
Success looks like: The Finance folder appears in the Files app under This PC.

2. Close the Files app, then open it again.
Success looks like: The Finance folder now appears and opens.

3. Sign out of Windows (do not just lock your screen).
Success looks like: You are fully signed out and returned to the sign-in screen.

4. Wait 1 minute.
Success looks like: One full minute has passed before the next sign-in.

5. Sign in again using your normal work account.
Success looks like: Your desktop loads normally.

6. Open the Files app and select This PC.
Success looks like: You can see the Finance folder shown.

7. Open the Finance folder.
Success looks like: Finance folders open without an error message.

## Contact the Service Desk if...

Contact the Service Desk if the Finance folder is still missing or still will not open after completing all steps above.

## Include these details in your ticket

- Your full name
- Your team (Finance)
- Your computer name (for example: DESKTOP-FB041)
- The time the issue started
- What you see (missing drive or error message)
- The exact words in the message, if shown
- Confirmation that you completed all 7 steps above
- Whether colleagues nearby have the same issue
