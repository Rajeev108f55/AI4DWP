# My New Laptop or Computer Isn't Starting Up Properly — What to Do
**Version:** 1.1 | **Date:** 11/08/2026 | **Status:** Draft

---

## Don't Worry — Your Files and Accounts Are Safe

If your new laptop or computer seems stuck on the getting-ready screens, nothing has been lost. Your emails, files, and account are all stored securely and are completely unaffected. This is a known issue that our IT team can fix quickly.

---

## What You Might See

- The getting-ready screens appear to have stopped or an error message appears
- You are unable to sign in or reach your normal work screen
- The getting-ready screens have been showing for more than 30 minutes without moving on

---

## What You Can Safely Do Yourself

**Step 1 — Wait a few minutes**  
Sometimes this takes longer than expected, especially if the computer is downloading the software it needs. Give it up to 30 minutes before doing anything else.

**Step 2 — Check your internet connection**  
Make sure the computer is connected to your workplace Wi-Fi or plugged in with an internet cable (the wider cable that plugs into the wall). If you are using an internet cable, check it is plugged in firmly at both ends.

**Step 3 — Restart the computer once**  
If the screen has completely stopped responding for more than 30 minutes, it is safe to hold the power button until the computer turns off, then press it again to turn it back on. Wait to see if it carries on by itself.

**Step 4 — If it fails again after restarting — stop here**  
Do not try restarting more than once. Do not attempt to skip any screens or enter any settings. Further steps need to be carried out by the IT team and doing more could slow down the fix.

---

## Contact the Service Desk If…

- The getting-ready screens stop, show an error, or do not finish after following the steps above
- The computer restarts but the same error appears again
- You are unable to reach your normal work screen or sign in to any work system

---

## What to Tell the Service Desk

When you call or log a call, please have the following ready — it will help the team fix this faster:

- **Your name and department**
- **The serial number** (a sticker printed underneath your laptop, or on the back or side of your computer — it usually starts with letters followed by numbers)
- **What you saw on screen** — even a rough description helps (e.g. "it showed an error message and stopped")
- **Approximately how long the getting-ready screens were showing** before it stopped
- **Whether the computer has been used before** or if it is brand new to you
