# My New Device Isn't Setting Itself Up — What to Do
**Version:** 1.0 | **Date:** 11/08/2026 | **Status:** Draft

---

## Don't Worry — Your Files and Accounts Are Safe

If your new device seems stuck during setup, nothing has been lost. Your emails, files, and account are all stored securely and are completely unaffected. This is a known setup issue that our IT team can fix quickly.

---

## What You Might See

- The device appears to be setting up but then stops or shows an error screen
- You are unable to sign in or reach your normal desktop
- The setup process has been running for more than 30 minutes without finishing

---

## What You Can Safely Do Yourself

**Step 1 — Wait a few minutes**  
Sometimes setup takes longer than expected, especially if the device is downloading updates. Give it up to 30 minutes before doing anything else.

**Step 2 — Check your internet connection**  
Make sure the device is connected to your office network or your approved home internet connection. If you are using a network cable, check it is plugged in firmly at both ends.

**Step 3 — Restart the device once**  
If setup has been completely frozen for more than 30 minutes with no movement on screen, it is safe to hold the power button until the device turns off, then press it again to restart. Wait to see if setup resumes on its own.

**Step 4 — If it fails again after restarting — stop here**  
Do not try restarting more than once. Do not attempt to skip any screens or enter any settings. Further steps need to be carried out by the IT team and doing more could slow down the fix.

---

## Contact the Service Desk If…

- Setup fails, freezes, or shows an error after following the steps above
- The device restarts but the same error appears again
- You are unable to reach your desktop or sign in to any work system

---

## What to Tell the Service Desk

When you call or raise a ticket, please have the following ready — it will help the team fix this faster:

- **Your name and department**
- **The device serial number** (usually a sticker on the underside of the laptop or back of the desktop)
- **What you saw on screen** — even a rough description helps (e.g. "it showed an error and stopped")
- **Approximately how long the setup was running** before it stopped
- **Whether the device has been used before** or if it is brand new to you
