# Floor 6 — Slow Login or Missing Desktop Icons? Here's What to Do

## What's happening
Some people on Floor 6 are seeing slow logins, or their usual desktop icons/folders
are missing this morning. Our team is already aware and actively working on it.

## Your data is safe
This issue does **not** affect your files, emails, or documents. Nothing has been
lost or deleted — this is a technical hiccup with how your computer sets things up
when you log in, not a data problem.

## What you can safely try yourself

1. **Wait a little and try logging in again.** Sometimes it just needs a second attempt.
2. **If you're already logged in and things look wrong (missing icons, etc.), sign out and sign back in once.** This often fixes it.
3. **Avoid restarting your computer over and over** — one sign-out/sign-in attempt is enough. If it doesn't help, stop and contact the service desk (see below).
4. **Keep working on anything that is working normally.** You don't need to stop using your computer entirely — just note down anything that looks off.

## Please do not

- Do not try to reinstall anything or change settings yourself.
- Do not ignore anything that looks unusual on your screen — especially if you see information that doesn't look like it belongs to you (for example, a document or client name you don't recognise). If you see this, stop and contact the service desk straight away.

## Contact the service desk if:

- Logging in takes more than a few minutes even after trying again
- Your desktop icons/shortcuts are still missing after signing out and back in once
- You see any information on your screen that doesn't seem like it should be yours
- You're simply not sure and want to check

## What to mention when you contact the service desk

Please include:
- Your name and the floor/desk you're sitting at (Floor 6)
- The approximate time you noticed the problem
- What you saw exactly (e.g., "login took 10 minutes", "desktop icons missing", "saw something that didn't look right")
- Whether signing out and back in helped or not
- Your computer/device name if you know it (not required)

We appreciate your patience while we get this sorted — thank you for reporting what
you're seeing, it genuinely helps us fix it faster.
