# Triage Summary — Copilot Showing Unauthorized Client Matter Data

## Summary (one line)
A paralegal reports Copilot displayed a client matter she should not have access to, indicating a potential data access/security breach.

## Impact (who / how many / business urgency)
- **Who:** One paralegal reported so far — identity to confirm; whether other users are affected — to confirm
- **How many:** 1 confirmed report; broader scope unknown — to confirm
- **Business urgency:** High — unauthorized access to client matter data is a potential confidentiality/data breach and may carry regulatory/client trust implications

## Known Facts
- A paralegal saw a client matter in Copilot that she states she has never had access to

## Missing Information to Gather
- Which client matter was exposed, and which client/matter the paralegal should legitimately have access to — to confirm
- The paralegal's assigned permission groups/roles and whether they were recently changed — to confirm
- Whether this is reproducible (can it happen again on demand) — to confirm
- Whether any data was copied, shared, or acted upon as a result of the exposure — to confirm
- Whether other users have experienced similar unauthorized data exposure in Copilot — to confirm
- Whether this correlates with a recent change (e.g., permissions update, app rollout, Copilot/SharePoint indexing change) — to confirm
- Timestamp of when the exposure occurred and was reported — to confirm

## Likely Category
Data security incident — potential unauthorized access/data leakage via Copilot (permissions/access control issue), to be confirmed by investigation.

## Suggested First Diagnostic Step
Escalate immediately to the security/data protection team to investigate the paralegal's permission assignments and Copilot/SharePoint access scope, treating this as a potential security incident pending confirmation.
