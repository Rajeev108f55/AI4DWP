# Escalation — Copilot Unauthorized Client Matter Access Incident

## The Copilot Incident — Handled Correctly

**What it actually is:**
This is **not** a support/helpdesk ticket — it's a **potential data security/access control incident**. A user viewing client matter data she should not have permission to see indicates a possible permissions misconfiguration, broken access control, or data leakage via the Copilot/SharePoint indexing layer. This falls under information security and data protection governance, not routine IT troubleshooting.

**What you would NOT do:**
Do **not** close this as "AI weirdness," a Copilot hallucination, or a UI glitch. Doing so risks dismissing a genuine unauthorized access event, leaves the underlying permissions issue unresolved, and could constitute a failure to report a data breach if the exposure is confirmed.

**Two-sentence escalation draft:**
> "A paralegal on Floor 6 has reported that Copilot displayed a client matter she does not have authorized access to, immediately following Friday's document management app rollout. This requires urgent investigation by security/data protection to confirm the scope of exposure and review her permission group assignments before any further use of Copilot on that floor."
