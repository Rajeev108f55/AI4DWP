# Copilot Prompting Starter Guide — FinBridge Staff

**Date:** 2026-08-12
**Audience:** All FinBridge office staff (non-technical)

---

Copilot works best when you treat it like a helpful colleague you need to give clear instructions to — not a search engine and not a mind reader. This guide gives you real, ready-to-use examples for Outlook, Word, and Excel, plus a simple formula to build your own prompts.

---

## The "Good Prompt" Formula

There's no way to "get it wrong" with Copilot — worst case, you just ask again. But here's a simple way to get a better answer first time. Just think of three things: **What do you want, what should it look at, and how should it look?**

> **"[What you want], based on [what to look at], as [how you want it]."**

Example: *"Summarise this email thread about the Q3 board meeting as 3 bullet points."*

That's genuinely it — you don't need special words or technical language. If the result isn't quite right, just ask again in plain English, e.g. *"Shorter, please"* or *"Make it more formal."* Copilot won't mind, and there's no limit on how many times you can ask.

---

## Outlook Prompts

1. *"Summarise this email thread in 3 bullet points, focusing on what decisions were made."*
2. *"Draft a polite reply declining this meeting invite and suggesting Thursday afternoon instead."*
3. *"Give me a summary of what I missed in my inbox this morning, grouped by urgency."*

---

## Word Prompts

1. *"Summarise this document in one paragraph for someone who hasn't read it."*
2. *"Rewrite this paragraph in a more formal tone for a client-facing report."*
3. *"Create a bullet-point list of action items mentioned in this document."*

---

## Excel Prompts

1. *"Explain what this formula in cell D4 is doing, in plain English."*
2. *"Look at column B and column C and tell me how much the numbers have gone up or down, as a percentage."*
3. *"Summarise the key trends in this data as 3 short bullet points."*

---

## A Quick Reminder on Data Safety

The good news first: Copilot inside Microsoft 365 is set up to work safely within FinBridge — it only ever looks at things you already have permission to see, so for your everyday emails, documents, and spreadsheets, you can use it with confidence.

The one habit worth carrying over from Day 1: if something feels especially sensitive — think client financial details or board-level material — just pause and ask your manager or IT first if you're not sure. It's a quick check, not a barrier, and it's the same good habit you'd use with any work tool.

For the vast majority of everyday tasks, Copilot is simply a helpful assistant that saves you time — so please do use it and get comfortable with it.
