# Leadership Update — Windows 11 Migration

**Date:** 2026-08-12
**Audience:** Leadership / Non-Technical Stakeholders

---

The move to Windows 11 has gone smoothly for most of the workforce, and the experience keeps improving each week. The issues that caused the most disruption early on — people unable to sign in, being locked out of their accounts, losing access to important files, and dropped calls from an unstable connection — have all been fixed, and staff have confirmed in their own words that these problems are behind them. Overall satisfaction is rising, with many employees now saying the new setup feels faster and more reliable than before. A few minor annoyances in the first week, such as changed icon layouts or wallpaper, caused brief confusion but were not raised again, suggesting people settled in quickly once they got used to the new look.

Two issues still need attention. Printing on one floor has not been fully fixed after several weeks, and the affected team's frustration is growing, so we are pushing for a permanent repair rather than relying on the current workaround. Separately, a new problem surfaced this week: a widely used spreadsheet program is crashing when people work with very large files, occasionally causing lost work. Because this is starting to affect a growing number of people's day-to-day productivity, we are treating it with the same urgency as the printing issue and have started an investigation. We are also following up directly with a small number of staff whose earlier concerns about shared file access have not been mentioned again, to confirm those are truly resolved rather than assuming so. We will keep monitoring progress on all of these and share updates as they close out.
