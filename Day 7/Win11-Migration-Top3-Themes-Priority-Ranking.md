# Win11 Migration - Top 3 Themes to Act on Today

Date: 2026-08-12
Scope: Ranking based on both impact severity and affected volume from 50 FinBridge end-user comments.

## Ranking Method
- Impact first: `Blocker` themes are prioritized over `Friction`, `Minor`, and `Positive` themes.
- Volume second: Within same severity, counts are used only after evaluating business criticality and whether users are fully blocked.

## Top 3 Priorities

### 1) AVD Sign-In / Account Lockouts (Count: 7, Severity: Blocker)
Why this ranks #1:
- Highest volume among all blocker themes.
- Prevents users from working at all (hard stop).
- Repeated pattern across the week suggests ongoing instability, not isolated incidents.

One sentence for manager:
- "AVD sign-in and account lockouts are our top priority because they are the most frequent blocker and directly stop staff from starting work."

### 2) OneDrive Missing/Inaccessible Files (Count: 4, Severity: Blocker)
Why this ranks #2:
- This is a true work-stopper tied to critical deliverables and meeting deadlines.
- The business risk is higher than issues with practical workarounds because users cannot retrieve required files.
- It outranks higher-volume themes where temporary alternatives exist.

One sentence for manager:
- "OneDrive missing-file incidents are our second priority because they create immediate blocker-level risk to deadlines and client-facing deliverables."

### 3) Shared Drive (S:) Access Issues (Count: 3, Severity: Blocker)
Why this ranks #3:
- Even at lower volume, affected users are fully blocked from core finance workflows.
- Comments explicitly tie this to month-end reporting, making the business timing risk high.
- It outranks higher-count issues where manual alternatives are available.

One sentence for manager:
- "S: drive access is third priority because each incident is a hard blocker on finance reporting work, especially around month-end timelines."

## Notes
- Floor 3 printer mapping (Count: 6) is high volume and still urgent, but ranked after the top 3 because a workaround exists (printing from another floor).
- VPN instability (Count: 4) should be handled in the same action window, with priority based on teams that are call-dependent.
