# Executive Summary — Windows 11 Migration Update

**Date:** 2026-08-12
**Audience:** Leadership / Non-Technical Stakeholders

---

Overall, the move to Windows 11 has gone well for the majority of staff, and things have continued to improve week over week. The most disruptive early issues — people being unable to sign in, getting locked out of their accounts, losing access to files, and unstable remote connections during calls — have all been resolved, and staff have confirmed this in their own feedback. Confidence in the new setup is rising, with many employees now describing the experience as smooth and even faster than before. A handful of smaller, cosmetic changes (things like icon positions or wallpaper) caused brief confusion in week one but have not come up again, suggesting people adjusted quickly.

Two things remain open. First, printing on one floor is still not working properly after several weeks, and frustration is building among the team affected — we are treating this as a priority and are escalating it for a permanent fix rather than continuing with a temporary workaround. Second, a new issue has emerged this week: a widely used spreadsheet tool is crashing when staff work with large files, in some cases causing people to lose unsaved work. Because this affects day-to-day productivity for a growing number of people, we are investigating it with urgency and treating it with the same priority as the printing issue. We're also double-checking a couple of areas where we haven't had enough recent feedback to be fully confident they're resolved, rather than assuming they are. We'll continue to monitor closely and provide updates as these items are worked through.
